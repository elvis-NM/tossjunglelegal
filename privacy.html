Privacy Policy

Toss Jungle Games (Stone Toss, Word Toss, Word Play) · Last updated: 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Toss Jungle Games app (“the app”, “we”, “us”), published by ElvisMakia, collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: if you create an account, your email address, user name and password. Passwords are handled by our backend provider (PlayFab) and are never visible to us. You can also play as a guest without an account.
  • Device identifier: a random ID created by the app so guest progress and game settings can be saved.
  • Gameplay activity: levels started, completed and failed, scores, coins, items viewed, bought and equipped in the in-game store, screens opened, settings changed, and tutorial progress.
  • Words you make in Word Toss and Word Play, with the game, language and date. These power your personal Word Journal and lifetime word statistics.
  • Multiplayer: your display name, match results and the preset quick-chat phrases you send during online matches.
  • Purchases: when you buy coins or a Word Journal subscription, Apple or Google processes the payment. We receive a purchase receipt (product, date, subscription status) but never your card or bank details.
  • Framed print orders: if you order a framed print, the name, shipping address and email you enter so we can make and deliver your order. Payment is handled by Stripe or PayPal.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and the app does not show third-party advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game: save your progress, coins, store items and settings, and restore them on another device when you sign in.
  • To provide features you ask for: online matches and tournaments, leaderboards, the Word Journal, and framed-print orders.
  • To verify purchases and subscriptions.
  • To understand how the game is played (for example, which levels are too hard and which store items are popular) so we can improve it.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Services that process data for us

  • Microsoft PlayFab: accounts, saved game data, statistics, gameplay events and word history.
  • Photon (Exit Games): real-time online multiplayer.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • Stripe, PayPal and SendGrid: framed-print payments and order emails.

These providers process data only to deliver their service and under their own privacy policies.

4. How long we keep it

Account and game data are kept while your account exists. Your word history keeps the last 13 months of words. Gameplay events are kept for up to 12 months and then deleted or anonymised. Order details are kept as long as needed for delivery and legal record-keeping.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can play as a guest without giving an email address.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account and its data by emailing info@silverbackglobalstudios.com from the address on your account. We will reply within 30 days.
  • Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your Apple ID or Google Play account settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local law, which we respect.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond what is needed to save game progress. Parents who believe their child has given us personal information can contact us to have it deleted.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with our providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes will be announced in the app.
