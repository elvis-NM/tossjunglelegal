Delete Your Account

Collect Pebbles (Stone Toss, Word Toss, Word Play) · by ElvisMakia / Silverback Global Studios · Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can delete your Collect Pebbles account and the data linked to it at any time, either in the app or by email.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Collect Pebbles and sign in.
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  4. Tap DELETE FOREVER to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

If you no longer have the app, email info@silverbackglobalstudios.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Collect Pebbles account” and your user name. We will delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account: user name, email address and password.
  • Coins, store items (backgrounds, hands, stones) and settings saved to your account.
  • Level progress, scores, online wins and tournament statistics, and your place on leaderboards.
  • Your word history and Word Journal.

What is kept

  • Gameplay events (levels played, screens opened and similar) already recorded are deleted or anonymised within 12 months, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases: payment records are held by Google Play, Apple, Stripe or PayPal under their own rules. The Full Game purchase stays with your Google Play / App Store account.
  • Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a Word Journal subscription. Cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or your App Store account settings.
  • Framed print orders: order details are kept as long as needed for delivery and legal record-keeping.
